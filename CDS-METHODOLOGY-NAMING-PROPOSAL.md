# CDS METHODOLOGY NAMING PROPOSAL

## The Need

The core innovation of CDS is the symbiotic pairing of **hardwired mechanical rules** with **AI judgment and creativity**. Neither works alone. Together they scale infinitely without losing consistency.

This methodology currently has no name. It's referred to descriptively as "symbiotic hardcoding + AI" or "hard-code + AI symbiosis."

We need a single name that:
- ✅ Captures the symbiotic nature (hard-code + AI together, not separate)
- ✅ Is memorable and speakable (people can say it)
- ✅ Works in governance documents (formal and professional)
- ✅ Is unique to CDS (not a generic term)
- ✅ Aligns with existing CDS naming (we use CORE, CoreSpine, CoreCycles, etc.)

---

## Naming Options

### Option 1: **CAST** (Code-AI Symbiotic Technology)

**Pronunciation:** "Cast" (like casting a spell, or casting metal)
**Full name:** CAST — Code-AI Symbiotic Technology
**Usage:** "We're using CAST methodology" / "This follows CAST principles"

**Pros:**
- Short, easy to say
- Technical-sounding without being jargon
- "Casting" implies creation + hardening (code + structure)
- Memorable
- Unique to CDS

**Cons:**
- Doesn't immediately convey "symbiotic"
- Acronym is less descriptive than descriptive term

**Examples in documents:**
- "This system uses CAST methodology: hard-code defines structure, AI executes creatively"
- "CAST ensures consistency (hard-code) and flexibility (AI) simultaneously"

---

### Option 2: **HYBRID** (formalized as "Hybrid Enforced Rigidity with Intelligent Distributed Execution")

**Pronunciation:** "Hybrid"
**Full name:** HYBRID Methodology
**Usage:** "This follows HYBRID principles" / "HYBRID enforcement pattern"

**Pros:**
- Explicitly says "hybrid" (hard + soft combined)
- Universally understood (people know hybrid)
- Immediately conveys balance

**Cons:**
- Very generic (hybrid is used everywhere in tech)
- Not unique to CDS (could describe many systems)
- Doesn't convey the specific symbiotic nature
- Lacks personality

**Examples in documents:**
- "CDS uses a HYBRID approach: hard mechanical rules + AI judgment"

---

### Option 3: **COHERE** (Code-Orchestrated Harmony in Execution and Research)

**Pronunciation:** "Co-here" (like "cohere" = stick together)
**Full name:** COHERE — Code-Orchestrated Harmony in Execution and Research
**Usage:** "COHERE methodology" / "Coherent hard-code and AI integration"

**Pros:**
- "Cohere" literally means "stick together" (perfect metaphor)
- Unique and memorable
- Sophisticated-sounding
- Double meaning: coherence (consistency) + cohere (stick together)
- Aligns with academic/research tone

**Cons:**
- Slightly abstract (may need explanation first time)
- COHERE pronunciation might be unclear to some (Co-here? Co-hare?)
- Acronym is complex

**Examples in documents:**
- "COHERE ensures that hard rules and AI creativity stick together"
- "The COHERE pattern: mechanical structure + intelligent execution"

### Option 4: **SHARD** (Symbiotic Hard Rule Adaptive Design)

**Pronunciation:** "Shard"
**Full name:** SHARD — Symbiotic Hard Rule Adaptive Design
**Usage:** "SHARD methodology" / "Following SHARD principles"

**Pros:**
- Explicitly includes "Symbiotic" and "Hard Rule" in acronym
- "Shard" = piece + strong (like glass/crystal shards — durable yet can refract differently)
- Unique metaphor
- Clear what it stands for

**Cons:**
- Metaphor (shards) may not be obvious
- Four-word acronym is harder to remember
- "Shard" has negative connotations (broken piece)

**Examples in documents:**
- "SHARD ensures each piece (rule) is hard, but together they adapt"

### Option 8: **CORE-LAB** (Code-Orchestrated Rigorous Enforcement — Locked AI Bridge)

**Pronunciation:** "Core-Lab"
**Full name:** CORE-LAB
**Usage:** "CORE-LAB methodology" / "Following CORE-LAB patterns"

## RECOMMENDATION

**🎯 STRONGEST CANDIDATE: CAST**

**Why CAST:**

1. **Short & Memorable:** One syllable, easy to say, impossible to forget
2. **Professional:** Works in formal documents and casual conversation
3. **Metaphor Alignment:** "Casting" = hardening + forming (like metal or concrete)
4. **Unique:** No other tech system uses CAST for this concept
5. **Scalable:** Can say "CAST principles," "CAST pattern," "CAST enforcement"
6. **CDS DNA:** If approved, becomes part of CDS identity going forward

**Secondary candidates:**
- **COHERE** (if you like more sophisticated, implies "sticking together")
- **NEXUS** (if you prefer abstract term for "connection point")

**NOT recommended:**
- **HYBRID** (too generic, used everywhere)
- **SHARD** (negative connotation, forced metaphor)
